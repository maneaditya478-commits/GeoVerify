# Benchmark Dataset & Provenance (Phase 4)

## 1. Overview

The GeoVerify Benchmark Dataset (`evaluation/datasets/benchmark.json` and `evaluation/datasets/benchmark.csv`) consists of **1,065 standardized test cases** representing real-world Indian address patterns, administrative edge cases, regional scripts, and stress-test scenarios.

All records are deterministic, reproducible, and verifiable under Apache/MIT and open government licensing.

---

## 2. Dataset Provenance & Data Privacy

### 2.1 Authoritative Base Sources
The geographic entities (States, Districts, Sub-Districts/Talukas, Localities/Villages, and PIN codes) are grounded in official public gazetteers:
1. **Local Government Directory (LGD)** — Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Government of India (**GODL-India**).
2. **All India Pincode Directory** — Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications (**GODL-India**).
3. **OpenStreetMap India Admin Boundaries** — Geofabrik & OpenStreetMap Contributors (**ODbL**).

### 2.2 Privacy Compliance (Zero PII Guarantee)
In accordance with ethical data privacy guidelines:
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: No individual names, phone numbers, email addresses, or personal identity numbers are present.
- **No Private Residential Coordinates**: Geographic coordinates reflect official administrative centroids, post office coordinates, or public commercial landmarks (e.g. World Trade Center, Connaught Place).
- **Synthetic Derivations**: Test cases are systematically generated by applying controlled real-world linguistic transformations, noise patterns, and administrative permutations onto public gazetteer entities.

```json
{
  "id": "BENCH-0001",
  "input_address": "World Trade Center, Kharadi, Haveli, Pune, Maharashtra 411014",
  "source_type": "SYNTHETIC",
  "category": "CORRECT_COMPLETE",
  "language": "EN",
  "script": "LATIN",
  "ground_truth": {
    "state": "Maharashtra",
    "district": "Pune",
    "subdistrict": "Haveli",

    "latitude": 18.552,
    "longitude": 73.939,
    "hierarchy_complete": true,

    "expected_status": "VERIFIED",
    "expected_completeness_level": "COMPLETE"

  "notes": "Canonical IT corridor address with full administrative hierarchy."

```

## 5. Dataset Generation & Extension

To regenerate or scale the benchmark dataset:

```powershell
# Generate standard 1,065 test cases
python -m evaluation.generate_dataset --size 1050 --seed 42

# Generate a custom sized benchmark dataset with custom seed
python -m evaluation.generate_dataset --size 5000 --seed 123 --output evaluation/datasets/benchmark_5k.json
```
